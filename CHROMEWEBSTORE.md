## Detailed Description
Semi-Trailer Axle Load & Road Compliance Checker (by KALES) helps logistics engineers, dispatchers, fleet managers, and trailer buyers verify whether a tractor-semi-trailer load legally complies with local road regulations before dispatch or purchase.

Key Features:
1. 20 National & Regional Road Law Compliance Checks: Verify per-axle, tandem/tridem group, kingpin, gross combination weight (GCW/GVW), length, width, and height limits across China (GB 1589-2016), EU (Directive 96/53/EC), United States (23 CFR 658.17 Federal Bridge Formula), Japan (Vehicle Restriction Order / Tokurei), Australia (NHVR HVNL General Mass Limits), Taiwan (Road Traffic Safety Rules Art. 38/79), Vietnam (Circular 39/2024/TT-BGTVT), Indonesia (PP 55/2012 & Zero ODOL MST 10 t), Peru (D.S. 058-2003-MTC RNV), Chile (D.S. 158 MOP), Tanzania (EAC Act 2016 / TANROADS), East Africa (EAC), Nigeria (Federal Highways / ECOWAS), Guinea (Code de la Route / ECOWAS), Ghana (L.I. 2180 / ECOWAS), South Africa (NRTR Reg. 240), Russia (PP No. 2200 10 t / 11.5 t), UAE (Cabinet Res. 138/2023), and Saudi Arabia (RGA), including abnormal-load permit & exemption guidance.
2. Lowboy, RGN, Hydraulic Modular & Steerable Jeep Dolly Semi-Trailer Profiles: Model fixed-gooseneck lowboy trailers, RGN detachable gooseneck trailers, multi-line hydraulic modular trailers, and 1–3 line steerable jeep dollies (18 t/line) with tractor fifth-wheel load split and swing clearances.
3. Suspension & Tyre Load Index Ratings: Compare mechanical leaf spring (13 t / 16 t axles), air suspension (10 t), interlinked hydraulic suspension, and speed-dependent hydraulic axle lines against ETRTO single/dual tyre load ratings.
4. Dump / Tipper Body Volume (m³ / cu yd) & Lift Sizing: Calculate U-type rear dump and side tipper cubic volume, bulk material density (coal, sand, gravel, iron ore), required side-wall height, and hydraulic lifting cylinder capacity.
5. Double-Deck Car Carrier (Auto Hauler) Simulator: Configure 6 / 8 / 10 car positions (12.9 / 17.1 / 22 m) under a 4.0 m overall height envelope with matching axle, rear overhang, and tractor presets.
6. Scaled Side-Elevation Schematic & Printable Spec Sheets: Inspect tractor steer/drive, dolly, trailer axle-group, kingpin, and CG loads visually with instant compliant / permit / not-feasible verdicts.
